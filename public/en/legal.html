Scope of Service

Local Local supports people, families, professionals, investors and companies that are evaluating, organizing or carrying out a project in Tandil. The service brings together local information, planning, professional judgment and coordination of a selected network. It is delivered in stages so that each decision prepares the next and the client keeps control of the process.

01. Personalized assessment and roadmap

Format: 100% remote.

We begin with a personalized video interview of approximately forty-five (45) minutes to understand the current situation, needs, priorities, timelines and decisions already made. We then analyze the project as a whole and identify what is already defined, what still needs to be resolved, which risks deserve attention and which alternatives may fit the client's reality.

Includes:

  • personalized video interview;
  • comprehensive review of the project;
  • identification of priorities, risks and critical decisions;
  • written assessment; and
  • roadmap with scenarios, criteria, next steps and recommended order of progress.

Delivery: within three (3) business days of the interview, provided the necessary information has been received. Current final price: USD 100. The assessment is an independent stage: after receiving it, the client may proceed on their own or engage a later stage.

Guarantee: if, within forty-eight (48) hours of receiving the document, the client considers that it did not provide clarity, relevant information or a useful roadmap, they may request a full refund of the amount paid for the assessment, specifying the reasons for their disagreement.

02. Strategic visit to Tandil and introductions to the local network

Format: in person. Estimated duration: one (1) to three (3) days, preferably between Monday and Wednesday.

Using the roadmap as our starting point, before the visit we design a personalized itinerary to see, compare and assess in Tandil the alternatives identified during the assessment. Depending on the project, it may include neighborhood tours, visits to schools or properties, and meetings with professionals, providers or institutions.

Includes:

  • advance design and coordination of the itinerary;
  • organization of visits, meetings and tours;
  • personal introductions to network members selected for the project;
  • final debrief and update of the roadmap; and
  • up to two (2) nights of accommodation, subject to availability and capacity.

Price: starting at USD 350 for two people. The final proposal is prepared after the assessment and depends on the number of people, the activities, the complexity of the itinerary and the accommodation available. For groups of more than two people, or when another accommodation option is required, the difference will be communicated in advance.

Transfers to Tandil and travel within the city are not included, unless the specific proposal states otherwise.

03. Comprehensive due diligence review

Format: hybrid. The review is conducted remotely, with in-person meetings when required by the transaction.

Once an alternative becomes concrete, we define the scope of the review according to the type and complexity of the transaction. The legal and notarial team analyzes the available documentation and background, the negotiated conditions, the contracts expected to be signed and the obligations to be assumed. When a technical assessment is needed, we coordinate the involvement of the relevant specialist.

Before work begins, the client receives a proposal that sets out:

  • what will be reviewed and what falls outside the analysis;
  • which professionals will be involved;
  • what documentation is needed;
  • estimated timelines;
  • our fees and third-party fees; and
  • additional expenses that can reasonably be anticipated.

Deliverable: comprehensive review report and risk map, including:

  • documentation and background reviewed;
  • risks, inconsistencies or observations identified;
  • possible impact of each point;
  • missing information or documentation; and
  • adjustments, safeguards or conditions recommended before proceeding.

The service includes a meeting to explain the findings and answer questions. With that information, the client decides whether to proceed, request adjustments, renegotiate conditions or discard the alternative. The review does not guarantee the outcome of the transaction and does not replace certifications, reports or technical controls that must be issued by licensed professionals.

Price: quoted individually based on the type, scope and complexity of the transaction. The fee is confirmed and agreed before work begins.

We know how complex it is to build an everyday life in a new city. That is why, once you are in Tandil, the client continues to have Local Local for general inquiries, recommendations and connections with people, institutions and trusted services.

The initial journey ends, but access to the network continues. This integration stage is included and has no expiry date for those who have completed the contracted process.

Integration includes:

  • general inquiries related to practical life in Tandil;
  • specific recommendations within the available network; and
  • initial connections with relevant people or services.

It does not include permanent availability, ongoing management, unlimited follow-up, representation, audits or new professional work. If an inquiry requires analysis, coordination or specific intervention, a new scope and quote will be defined before proceeding.
